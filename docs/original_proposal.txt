Project Title: Tetris
A condensed version of Tetris. 

Technical:
Basically classic tetris except limited speed up and no fancy mechanics 
(e.g. t-spin). I was thinking the grid is basically a matrix of flip-flops 
whose input is multiplexed between the FF above it, the FF above and to the 
right, and the FF above and to the left depending on what the player is 
inputting. For the pieces building up at the bottom, the flip-flops are 
disabled unless the line is completely full. If space is an issue I might
do a smaller grid.

I/Os:
Outputs:
- VGA: 8
- Audio: 1

Inputs: 
- Button for dropping: 1
- Button for storing: 1
- Button for exiting: 1
- Button for rotating: 1
- SPI joystick or buttons for x,y piece movement: 4

Hardware Peripherals:
- Tiny VGA PMOD + VGA display
- maybe Tiny Audio PMOD
- 4 buttons for dropping, storing, rotating, and exiting functionality
- SPI joystick or more buttons for x,y piece movement
